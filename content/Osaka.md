# PLACES TO VISIT - OSAKA
- **JoJo Ramen Restaurant** (https://maps.app.goo.gl/8fKj9RwMMbxuFp196)
  - Saw an IG of people waiting ~2h in line to have dinner. Better to go early to try to avoid line, but I'm personally fine with skipping this restaurant
- **Minami (Namba)** (https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e4001.html)
  - Southern downtown of Osaka. Abundant dining and shopping choices. Better to visit at night
- **Osaka Castle (Osakajo)** (https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e4000.html)
  - ~6.5eur entrance. Lines can be long on busy days, purchase e-ticket in advance to skip
- **Sumiyoshi Taisha** (https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e4007.html)
  - One of Japan's oldest shrines. Displays unique style of shrine architecture called Sumiyoshi-zukuri
- **Shinsekai** (https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e4012.html)
  - "New world". Nostalgia evoking district. Better to visit at night
- **Shitennoji Temple** (https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e4011.html)
  - One of the oldest temples in Japan
- **Kita (Umeda)** (https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e4009.html)
  - Northern downtown of Osaka. Offers abundance of shopping, dining and entertainment
- **~~Minoo Park~~** (https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e4019.html) 
  - Forested park with waterfall just outside Osaka
- **Kuromon Market** (https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e4031.html)
  - Popular market street for seafood, produce and more
- **Tenma** (https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e4024.html) 
  - District known for its shrine and shopping arcade
## AROUND OSAKA
- **Himeji Castle** (https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3501.html)
  - Japan's best preserved feudal castle. ~1h away from Osaka

## TRADITIONAL - OSAKA
- **Takoyaki** - grilled octopus 
- **Okonomiyaki** - pancake-like dish
- **Kushikatsu** - deep fried pieces of food on skewers
- **Kitsune Udon** - hot udon soup with deep fried tofu
- **Teppanyaki** - style of cooking a meal on a metal place in the presence of a customer
